Our story

Teaching that makes concepts click

Shubham Indani started Indani Coaching Classes in 2017 to give students in Dhamangaon Railway proper coaching in Maths and Science without travelling to a big city.

Classes start from the basics of each topic. We explain why every formula works, then give students plenty of structured practice. They score well in Board exams, and they keep what they learn for the next class.

  • Concept-first teaching instead of rote memorisation
  • Focused preparation for school and Maharashtra State Board exams
  • Motivational workshops and structured practice sessions
  • Personal attention for every student
